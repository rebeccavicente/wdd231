NP Membership

Fee: Free for registered non-profits.

  • Listing in the member directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership

Fee: R$ 300 per year (sample price).

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Business profile with logo in the directory
  • Discounted tickets to chamber events
  • Access to one training workshop per year

Silver Membership

Fee: R$ 600 per year (sample price).

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Eligible for Business Spotlights on the home page
  • Access to all training workshops
  • Shared posts on chamber social media

Gold Membership

Fee: R$ 1,200 per year (sample price).

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Priority spotlight placement on the home page
  • Event sponsorship and advertising opportunities
  • Free tickets to the quarterly business luncheon
